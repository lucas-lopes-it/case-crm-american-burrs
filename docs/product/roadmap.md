# Roadmap

The roadmap is organized by capability, not by date. Each capability is something the product
learns to do and that can be verified in the repository.

The direction comes from the [vision](../vision/vision.md): every new capability has to reduce what
the rep reads before acting.

## The product in production

The original system ran at American Burrs. These phases happened there and have no code here.

| Phase | What shipped | What it validated |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery | Lean Inception with the business areas | The professor as decision maker and the term-based cycle |
| MVP | Traffic light and briefing on top of a market CRM, with integrations built from scratch | That reps act on a ready-made briefing |
| Own product | CRM, pipeline and kit builder in our own low-code application | Over 60% reduction in commercial tool costs at the MVP stage, 80% at full rollout |

## Current focus

### Sprint 003: the layers around the code

Shipped in this sprint:

- vision, manifesto, principles and glossary in `docs/vision/`;
- this roadmap by capability;
- review, retrospective and learning for sprints 001 and 002 in `docs/sprints/`;
- license, code of conduct, security policy and contributing guide;
- pull request template, issue templates and continuous integration with typecheck, tests and evals
  in mock mode;
- the whole documentation in English, with a cover image for the social preview.

## Next

### Sprint 004: prompt v2

**Problem:** prompt v1 uses the same tone for a red account and a black account. Anticipating a kit
and winning back a professor who skipped the cycle are different conversations.

**Deliverable:** a prompt v2 with a distinct instruction per color, compared with v1 in the evals,
criterion by criterion.

**How we know it worked:** v2 keeps 100% on the guardrails and the difference in tone shows up across
the 20 accounts without increasing the fallback rate.

### Sprint 005: daily queue

**Problem:** when several accounts trigger on the same day, the rep doesn't know where to start.

**Deliverable:** ranking by revenue over the last 12 months, with tests.

### Sprint 006: measuring edits

**Problem:** the "briefings sent without edits" metric is still "to be instrumented".

**Deliverable:** recording the sent text next to the draft and computing the difference.

## Off the roadmap, on purpose

- Automatic message sending.
- Discounts or commercial terms generated by the AI.
- Predictive churn score in place of the days-without-purchase rule.
- Screens inside the ERP.

The reasons are in the [README](../../README.md#what-was-left-out-on-purpose).
