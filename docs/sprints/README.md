# Sprints

Each sprint leaves three documents:

- **review:** what shipped, story by story;
- **retrospective:** what worked, what could improve and what changes next time;
- **learning:** the concept the sprint taught, written for another product person, with an
  exercise.

| Sprint | Period | Theme | Documents |
|---|---|---|---|
| 001 | Oct 6, 2026 | Product rules as tested code | [review](sprint-001/review.md), [retrospective](sprint-001/retrospective.md), [learning](sprint-001/learning.md) |
| 002 | Oct 22 to Nov 18, 2026 | The case through a product lens | [review](sprint-002/review.md), [retrospective](sprint-002/retrospective.md), [learning](sprint-002/learning.md) |
| 003 | Nov 18, 2026 | Vision, ritual and governance | in progress, see the [roadmap](../product/roadmap.md) |

Reconstruction sprints don't have a fixed length. A sprint closes when the planned capability is on
`main`, with a test or document that proves it.
